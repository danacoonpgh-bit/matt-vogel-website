Meet Matt

Pittsburgh born. Built from the ground up.

Matt grew up in Bloomfield, went to Central Catholic and played baseball at Penn State Beaver. He spent more than 20 years in construction around Pittsburgh before bringing that experience to real estate with Coldwell Banker Realty.

His business runs on relationships and trust. Buying or selling a home is one of the biggest financial decisions you'll make, so Matt makes sure you understand every step. He even recommends talking with a few agents first, so you can choose the one you are most comfortable with.

Off the clock, you'll find him renovating investment properties, spending time with family, or following Pittsburgh sports.

The builder's edge

Most agents see paint and staging. Matt sees the bones.

Before real estate, Matt spent more than 20 years building and fixing homes across Pittsburgh. That background changes what you know before you sign anything.

01

Showings that go deeper

Roof, foundation, mechanicals, windows. Matt walks every home looking for what the listing photos don't show, so you find out before you fall in love.

02

Real numbers on repairs

"That needs work" isn't an answer. Matt gives you a realistic sense of what a fix costs, so your offer and your budget reflect the real house.

03

Sharper negotiating

When inspection results come back, Matt knows which items matter and what they're worth. That's leverage, whether you're buying or selling.
